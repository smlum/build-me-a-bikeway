About

What is this?

Build me a Bikeway helps Montrealers request new cycling infrastructure from their local elected officials — in two clicks.

How it works

  1. Click any road on the map
  2. See your borough's elected representative
  3. Choose an infrastructure type and copy the draft email
  4. Send it directly to your official

The bike network

The coloured lines show Montreal's existing cycling infrastructure, from protected lanes to shared roads. Use it to spot gaps near you.

Infrastructure types

Protected lane — physically separated from traffic by a barrier or curb.

Painted bike lane — a dedicated lane marked on the road surface.

Local street bikeway — a calmed residential street prioritising cyclists.

Multi-use path — a shared path for cyclists and pedestrians.

Data sources

Bike network: Données Montréal (CBICS dataset)

Borough contacts updated periodically from public sources.
